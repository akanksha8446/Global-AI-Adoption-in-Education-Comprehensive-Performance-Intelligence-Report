Dashboard Overview

What does this dashboard analyze?

Dashboard 2 focuses on deeper AI adoption and usage patterns within education. It brings together multiple analytical views to provide a broader understanding of how AI is being used by students, teachers and educational institutions.

  • Student versus teacher AI usage
