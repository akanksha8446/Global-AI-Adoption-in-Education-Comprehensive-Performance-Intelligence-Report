About This Story

This Tableau Story analyzes the adoption of artificial intelligence in education across different countries and regions. It examines geographic differences, historical growth, popular AI tools, and the urban-rural digital divide.

The objective is to understand how AI is transforming education and identify the major opportunities and challenges associated with its adoption.
